## Milestone 2 Instructions

### Overview

The goal of Milestone 2 is to produce a preliminary design document for your project. This document should describe the software components you plan to implement, how they will be parallelized, and how you will evaluate performance.

This is a planning phase. Your design is expected to evolve as you implement and profile your code. The purpose of this milestone is to demonstrate that you have thought carefully about the structure of your project and have a clear path forward.

### Report Content

Your report is expected to address each of the following topics.

1. **Project scope and problem definition.** Clearly define the computational problem you are solving. Describe the inputs, outputs, and any assumptions or simplifications. Explain what a successful project looks like from your perspective: what results do you expect to produce, and what would constitute a satisfying outcome?

2. **Survey of related work.** Summarize existing approaches to the same or similar problems. Cite relevant papers, libraries, or open-source projects. Explain how your approach relates to or differs from prior work.

   You are not expected to advance the state of the art. The focus of this project is on implementation, profiling, and performance analysis. Your design choices should nonetheless be informed by the existing literature, so that by the time you write the final report, you can explain and justify the algorithm and parallelization strategy you chose.

3. **Computational challenges.** Based on your understanding of the problem and the related work, identify the main technical challenges you anticipate and, where possible, your initial thoughts on how to address them.

4. **CUDA implementation plan.** Identify the computationally intensive parts of your algorithm that will be implemented as GPU kernels, even if the details may change as you progress.

5. **MPI implementation plan.** Describe how the workload will be distributed across multiple processes.

6. **Profiling and performance analysis plan.** Describe how you plan to measure and evaluate the performance of your code. What experiments do you intend to run, and what do you hope to learn from them?

7. **Timeline.** Provide a week-by-week plan for the remaining milestones. For each week, list the specific tasks you plan to complete. Be realistic — account for debugging, testing and optimization, and report writing.

### Submission

Submit a report of approximately 2 pages (excluding references). The report should be well-organized, clearly written, and sufficiently detailed that a reader can understand what you plan to build and how you plan to evaluate it.
